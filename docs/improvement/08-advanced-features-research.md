# Advanced Chess Features Research

> Aspect 9 of the menu. Long-term missions that can take the application to
> another level.

## Long-term themes

- **Opening intelligence.** A real opening analysis system: find theoretical
  errors, compare moves with databases, build a personalised repertoire.
- **Tactical pattern recognition.** Detect forks, pins, skewers, sacrifices,
  mates, unprotected pieces and missed combinations.
- **Endgame coach.** Detect endgames, identify technical errors, use Syzygy
  tablebases and create endgame exercises.
- **Player intelligence.** Build a detailed playing profile, identify habits
  and measure the player's real progress.
- **Human-like opponent analysis.** Study Maia and other models that predict
  human moves, to understand not only the best move but also the likely
  errors at the player's level.

## The research mission

Research in depth the advanced features that could improve Chess Coach AI.

Study these areas:

1. Opening preparation and personalised repertoires.
2. Tactical pattern recognition.
3. Endgame tablebases and endgame training.
4. Chess position evaluation.
5. Human-like chess models such as Maia.
6. Personalised learning.
7. Spaced repetition.
8. Automatic exercise generation.
9. Opponent preparation.
10. Long-term performance tracking.

For each area, identify the best open-source projects, scientific
publications, libraries and technical approaches.

Compare their value with the current features of the application.

Propose a prioritised roadmap, and implement only the improvements that fit
our architecture.
