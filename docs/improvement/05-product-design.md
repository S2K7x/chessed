# Product Design — chessed UX Upgrade

> Aspect 6 of the menu.

Act as a Senior Product Designer specialised in chess applications.

Audit every screen and every interaction of Chess Coach AI.

Compare our user experience with Chess.com and Lichess.

Analyse:

- Visual hierarchy.
- Board readability.
- Navigation between moves.
- Presentation of the classifications.
- Evaluation graph.
- Coach panel.
- Display of the lines.
- Game import.
- Responsive design.
- Accessibility.
- Dark and light modes.
- Visual feedback.
- Animations.
- Loading and error states.

Identify what makes the application hard, confusing or needlessly complex to
use.

The design must be professional, minimalist and consistent.

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards.

Do not just change colours or corner radii: genuinely improve the workflows.

Implement the most important improvements and check that they work on desktop
and on mobile (screenshots at 390 px wide, and the accessibility audit in
`e2e/a11y.spec.ts`).
