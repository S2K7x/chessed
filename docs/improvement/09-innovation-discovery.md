# chessed — Innovation & Product Discovery

> Aspect 10 of the menu. Finds the features nobody has thought of yet.

Think like the founder of a start-up specialised in chess learning
technology.

Do not limit yourself to the features already in the roadmap.

The goal: discover features we have not thought of yet, which could make Chess
Coach AI much more useful for improving at chess.

Search:

- Scientific publications on learning chess.
- Research on cognitive mechanisms and memorisation.
- Recent GitHub projects.
- Competing applications.
- Reddit communities and chess forums.
- Work on chess engines and AI models.
- New teaching technologies.

Try in particular to answer this question:

> "If the goal were to make a chess player improve as fast as possible with
> software, which features would have to be built?"

Explore new ideas freely:

- Analysis of thinking habits.
- Detection of calculation errors.
- Exercises generated from the player's own games.
- Adaptive training.
- Position simulations.
- Preparation against opponents.
- Conversational coach.
- Analysis of decisions rather than moves alone.
- Measuring the quality of thinking processes.

For each idea, explain:

1. The problem it solves.
2. Its teaching value.
3. How it could work.
4. The technologies needed.
5. Its feasibility in our architecture.
6. Its cost and complexity.
7. Its potential impact on improvement.

Rank the ideas by real potential.

Then select the three most interesting innovations and propose how to fit them
into the roadmap.

Implement nothing before explaining the architectural changes required, and
wait for the user's agreement.

The key point: measure the progress of Chess Coach AI not by the number of
features added, but by its ability to make the player improve at chess.
